Privacy Policy for Merge it

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Merge it ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

The App stores gameplay data (such as your score, level, in-game currency, settings, and progress) locally on your device. This information is not personally identifiable and is not transmitted to us or any third party by the App itself.

The App requests permission to send notifications, used only to remind you to return to the game. You can disable this permission at any time in your device settings.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App may display advertisements provided by Unity Ads. Unity Ads may collect certain information, such as your advertising identifier, IP address, and device information, to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance. This data collection is governed by Unity's own Privacy Policy, available at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's settings.

3. Online Features and Data Storage

The App includes optional online features (weekly leaderboard, friends, and profile customization) powered by Google Firebase. To use these features, the App creates an anonymous account for your device and may store your chosen nickname, avatar, weekly score, and basic gameplay statistics on Firebase servers. This information is not linked to your real identity and is only visible to other players of the App as described within the App itself.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except as necessary for the advertising and online services described above.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information stored by the App. However, no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

7. Your Choices

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data through your device settings. To request deletion of your online profile data (nickname, avatar, stats), contact us at the email address below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
